# ⚡ SparsioQ: Algorithmic Quantitative Wealth & Portfolio Optimization Platform

SparsioQ is an institutional-grade quantitative wealth management platform designed to democratize advanced mathematical financial tools for retail investors starting with capital as low as ₹100.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **🔰 Zero-Knowledge Auto-Pilot Mode**: Designed for beginners with zero stock market knowledge. Plain-English AI guidance, live market weather checks, and 1-click execution.
- **⚡ Quant Pro Mode**: Full mathematical breakdown exposing Gaussian HMM state probabilities, 3D Tensor HoSVD factor loadings, $ CVXOPT efficient frontiers, and Qiskit Quantum Option Pricing circuits.
- **📲 Real Bank & UPI Integration**: NPCI Account Aggregator bank account linking, live UPI deep-links (upi://pay), and Razorpay Python SDK integration.
- **📡 Real-Time NSE Market Feed**: Live stock price streaming via yfinance API for NSE blue-chips.
- **📜 Regulatory & Terms Disclosure**: Built-in SEBI/RBI investor risk disclosure & terms of service onboarding flow.
