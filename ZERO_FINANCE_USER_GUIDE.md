# 🔰 SparsioQ: Zero-Finance Knowledge User Guide

Welcome to **SparsioQ**! This system is built so that **anyone**—even with zero background in finance, stock markets, or mathematics—can safely invest and grow their money in real time without any constraints.

---

## 🚀 How to Use SparsioQ in 3 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Open the App
Go to **[http://localhost:8501](http://localhost:8501)** in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).

### Step 2: Select "Beginner / Auto-Pilot Mode"
In the left sidebar, choose **🔰 Beginner / Auto-Pilot Mode (Zero Finance Knowledge)**. This removes all complex financial formulas and replaces them with simple, plain-English guidance.

### Step 3: Enter Your Investment & Click Invest
1. **Enter Amount**: Type how much money you want to invest (starting from as low as **₹100**).
2. **Choose Your Goal**: Select whether you want **Safe Growth**, **Balanced Growth**, or **Max Growth**.
3. **Click `🚀 INVEST MY ₹100 NOW`**: 
   - SparsioQ automatically fetches live stock prices from the National Stock Exchange (NSE).
   - It checks the market weather to ensure there is no crash.
   - It picks the **4 healthiest, eco-friendly companies** (HDFC Bank, Reliance, TCS, Infosys).
   - It slices your order into micro-chunks so brokers don't charge you hidden fees.
